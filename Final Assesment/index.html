Welcome to Cricket the World

Step into the exciting world of cricket, one of the most popular and celebrated sports on the planet.
Played by millions of people across more than 100 countries, cricket is a sport full of strategy, skill,
and rich traditions.

Why Learn About Cricket?

Cricket is a team sport played between two sides of 11 players using a bat and a ball.
Whether you are watching a high-speed 3-hour match or a traditional 5-day Test match, cricket
offers non-stop strategy and dramatic athletic moments.

Explore the Site

  • History: Learn where cricket started and how it grew into a global phenomenon.
  • Rules: Master the core laws of the game, scoring methods, and player dismissals.
  • Gallery: Browse classic imagery of essential gear, pitch setups, and massive stadiums.
  • Did you know? Cricket is estimated to be the second most popular spectator sport in the world, right behind soccer!
