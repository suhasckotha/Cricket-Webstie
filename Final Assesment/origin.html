ORIGIN & HISTORY

Early Beginnings

Cricket began hundreds of years ago in rural England. Historical records suggest that it started as a children's
game in the southeastern countryside during the 16th century. By the 17th century, adults began playing, and local
matches turned into organized competition.

Global Expansion

Throughout the 18th and 19th centuries, cricket spread internationally alongside British trade routes.
Countries such as India, Australia, South Africa, New Zealand, and the West Indies embraced the game,
making it a central part of their sporting culture

Key Historical Milestones

  • 1744: The first official written "Laws of Cricket" were published.
  • 1787: The Marylebone Cricket Club (MCC) was founded, becoming the official guardian of cricket rules.
  • 1877: The first official international Test match took place between England and Australia in Melbourne.
  • 1971: The first One Day International (ODI) was played, introducing faster, single-day matches to modern audiences.
